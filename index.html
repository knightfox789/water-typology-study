Building Informed and Scalable Water Security Solutions for Gujarat

Gujarat's seven regions differ in rain, rock and soil, and so in how much water they can hold and how much they use. Development Support Centre's 2020–22 study estimated both, region by region and district by district.

A study by Development Support Centre, supported by Hindustan Unilever Foundation, in collaboration with DSC Foundation, ACWADAM and INREM Foundation. Interactive edition by Kaushal Gadariya.
