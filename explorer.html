    Agricultural demand

    The study gives agricultural water demand two ways, and the gap looks very different under each. As recommended uses the crop water requirements in the State Irrigation Plan. As actually used uses what farmers reported applying in the study's 2020–22 farmer survey, about a fifth more across Gujarat. The study's summary uses actual use, so the explorer opens with it.

    Demand threshold

    Each district is classed high or low demand by comparing its water demand per hectare of cropped land with one value: 0.0030 MCM a hectare, the value the study used. The average across districts is 0.0049, and at the study's value nearly every district reads high, so the classes separate districts weakly. Move the slider to try a stricter or looser value; the count, the map and the cards follow.
